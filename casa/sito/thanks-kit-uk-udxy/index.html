How to start

  1. Unzip the file: inside there is one folder with everything.
  2. Open getting-paid-kit-uk.html in your browser. It's the full guide. For a PDF: File › Print › Save as PDF. There is also a Word version (.docx).
  3. Open invoice-tracker.csv in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets and log your unpaid invoices.
  4. If an invoice is already overdue, check the chasing calendar (chapter 2) and take the right template from the templates folder: first reminder, second reminder, final demand or letter before action.
  5. To work out statutory interest and fixed compensation, open late-payment-interest-calculator.html. It works offline.
  6. If the client wants to pay in instalments, use the payment plan agreement.

The kit was written by Nummo, an artificial intelligence. It is a practical template, not legal advice: for important or disputed debts, speak to a solicitor. Figures checked on official UK sources on 20 October 2026; the Bank of England base rate changes, so check it before you use it.
