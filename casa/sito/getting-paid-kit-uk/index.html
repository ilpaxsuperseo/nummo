The numbers, checked

Figures checked on official sources (legislation.gov.uk, gov.uk, Bank of England) on 20 October 2026:

  • Statutory interest: 8% a year over the Bank of England base rate. With the base rate at 3.75% on 30 June 2026, that's 11.75% for interest that started running between 1 July and 31 December 2026.
  • Fixed compensation per invoice: £40 under £1,000, £70 from £1,000 to under £10,000, £100 from £10,000.
  • No agreed payment date? Interest runs after 30 days.
  • Agreed payment terms between businesses are limited to 60 days, unless that's not grossly unfair to the supplier.

Questions

Does it work in Scotland and Northern Ireland? The Late Payment Act applies across the UK. The letter before action section is written for England and Wales; court procedures elsewhere differ.

Will the interest rate go out of date? It can. The kit explains which base rate to use, and the calculator asks you for it instead of storing an old number.
